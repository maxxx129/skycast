How it works

Crawls on every visit. Pulls daily history since 1980 (ECMWF ERA5), the last 92 days of observations and the 16 day live forecast (NOAA GFS, ECMWF, DWD ICON), and NOAA's El Niño / La Niña index.

Learns each city. Gradient boosted decision trees study about 100,000 examples of "the weather looked like this, what happened h days later?" for every lead time up to a year, and retrain daily.

Tests itself honestly. Before trusting itself it forecasts the last three years blind and compares against 30 year normals. Where it can't beat normals, it leans on them.

Corrects its own mistakes. Every outlook is logged. When those days arrive it measures its error and adjusts. Specific dates are reliable for about two weeks; beyond that, read answers as likely windows.

Data: Open-Meteo (ERA5, GFS, ECMWF, ICON), NOAA Climate Prediction Center. Everything runs in your browser.
